# Waterfowl vs. Precipitation at NCOS — Final Project

Authors: Nina Cutner, Kelsey Hammond, Sofia Favela

General Information
This repository contains the final project for our R-based data analysis course. We examine how precipitation influences waterfowl abundance at North Campus Open Space (NCOS), a restored coastal wetland in Santa Barbara, CA. Using bird survey data and NOAA daily weather records spanning water years 2020–2025, we produce four visualizations and a linear regression to explore temporal patterns, seasonal trends, and the lagged relationship between precipitation and waterfowl abundance.
Research questions:

What is the effect of the previous month's precipitation on total monthly waterfowl abundance at NCOS?
How does total waterfowl abundance vary through time at NCOS?
Are there seasonal patterns in waterfowl abundance and precipitation at NCOS?
